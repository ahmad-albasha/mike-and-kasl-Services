Meet Mike

With over 18 years of experience in the beauty industry, Mike has built his journey through passion, education, and creativity. From studying and traveling around the world to participating in international hair shows, events, and education, he has continuously pushed himself to evolve and master his craft.

Today, as a salon owner, hair artist, and educator, Mike combines technical expertise with a true passion for transformation — believing that great hair is not simply about beauty, but about confidence, personality, and making every woman feel her best.

Why Mike & Kals?

At Mike & Kals, beauty is personal.

We combine years of expertise, creativity, premium products, and personalized consultation to create results designed for you. From hair transformations and color to nails, beauty, and special occasions, every service is delivered with one philosophy: never compromise on quality, detail, or the health of your hair.

We don't want you to simply leave looking beautiful — we want you to leave feeling confident, understood, and excited to come back.
